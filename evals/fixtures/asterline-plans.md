# Asterline Plans (fictional evaluation document)

Asterline is a fictional team-notes service. The current Starter plan permits 1,000 API requests per hour per workspace. The hourly window resets on the hour in UTC. The Pro plan permits 6,000 API requests per hour per workspace.

Starter is free. Pro costs $29 per workspace per month when billed monthly. Annual Pro billing gives two months free. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.

Starter workspaces may store three documents, each at most 5 MB. Pro workspaces may store 20 documents, each at most 25 MB. Both plans accept PDF, TXT, and Markdown files.

Starter support replies within two business days. Pro support replies within four business hours. Neither support target is an uptime guarantee.

An archived 2024 policy listed 500 Starter API requests per hour. That limit was retired in January 2025; the current Starter limit is 1,000 per hour.
